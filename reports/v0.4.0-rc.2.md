### Upstream Suggestions (`google/ax` & `agent-substrate/substrate`)
- **`#689` / `#697` / `#735` (`ax local` Kind bring-up & resume)**: On Apple M5 + Podman, fresh `ax local start` takes ~128 s (serial readiness waits + dual Postgres, not image pulls) and `stop` $\rightarrow$ `start` takes 170–362 s due to pod-certificate volume mount backoff after `podcertificate-controller` restarts (`ate-local/LOCAL-BACKEND.md`). Parallelizing `ax-postgres` + control-plane rollouts and restarting failed-mount pods on resume would cut both substantially.
- **`#712` (Docker-free `ax local start`)**: Suggest auto-probing Podman's existing `<machine>-root` connection (`CONTAINER_CONNECTION=podman-machine-default-root`) on macOS instead of asking users to mutate their default VM with `podman machine set --rootful`, and consider a `local.Backend` seam above `cluster.Provider` for zero-Kubernetes local backends (`ate-local`).
- **`#740` (`launcher-done` EACCES)**: Confirmed in live `swarm/02` runs that non-snapshot tasks have `/workspace/.ax` owned by `1000:1000` (`0755`), while snapshot-seeded tasks have `root:root` — fixing ownership/privilege consistency lets one-shot launchers record completion reliably without `#738`'s wrapper workaround.
- **Proto wire-numbering note (`#770` right after `v0.4.0-rc.2`)**: `#770` (`651fd23`) renumbered `TaskSpec.gateway` (9 $\rightarrow$ 8), `TaskSpec.session_id` (10 $\rightarrow$ 9), and `TaskStatus.resolved_argv` (7 $\rightarrow$ 6) 6 minutes after `v0.4.0-rc.2` (`f4fe642`); flag CLI/server lockstep in `v0.4.0-rc.3` release notes.
